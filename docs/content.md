## Steps (original, translated, for reference; outside the build scope)

1. Wash the vegetables, herbs and meat and pat the meat dry with kitchen paper. Heat 300 ml [600 ml] water
   in the kettle. You will also need 1 small pot (with lid), kitchen paper, 1 large pan and 1 small bowl.
   Put 300 ml [600 ml] hot water into a small pot, salt it, bring to the boil once, add the basmati rice and
   simmer, covered, over low heat for approx. 10 min. Trim the ends of the sugar snap peas and cut into
   0.5 cm pieces. When the rice is done, add the sugar snap pieces and leave both to steep, covered, for
   another 10 min.
2. Trim the ends of the zucchini, halve it lengthwise and cut into approx. 0.5 cm thick half-moons. Peel the
   onion, halve it and cut into thin strips.
3. Press the chicken breasts a little flatter with the heel of your hand and season both sides with salt and
   pepper. Heat ½ tbsp [1 tbsp] oil in a large pan over medium heat and fry the chicken for 7–8 min. on each
   side. Remove from the pan and keep warm.
4. Pick the parsley leaves and chop finely. In a small bowl, mix ⅔ of the parsley, salt, pepper, mustard to
   taste and the yoghurt well.
5. Wipe out the pan with kitchen paper, heat 1 tbsp [2 tbsp] oil again over medium heat and fry the onion
   strips and zucchini pieces for 5–6 min. Season with za'atar, salt and pepper. Return the chicken to the
   pan to keep it warm.
6. Once cooked, fluff up the sugar snap basmati rice a little with a fork, then stir in ½ tbsp [1 tbsp]
   butter and divide between plates. Arrange the chicken and zucchini vegetables alongside, garnish with the
   parsley-mustard yoghurt and sprinkle with the remaining parsley. Enjoy your meal!
